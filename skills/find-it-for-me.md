# 幫我找 / Find it for me

在信件、行事曆與第二大腦裡找使用者要的東西。
先給最可能的那一封（主旨、寄件人、日期），再列其他候選。找不到就直說，建議換什麼關鍵字。

Find what the user is looking for across mail, calendar and Second Brain.
Lead with the most likely hit (subject, sender, date), then the other candidates.
If nothing matches, say so and suggest better search words.

唯讀。不要封存、貼標、寄信。
Read-only. Do not archive, label, or send.
