# 轉成投影片 / Turn into a slide

把一封信或一份整理結果變成簡短投影片：第一張是標題，之後每張一個重點、最多八行條列。
輸出是本機 HTML 檔（data/slides/），不上傳、不分享。

Turn an email or a skill result into a short deck: a title slide, then one point
per slide with at most eight bullets. The output is a local HTML file in
data/slides/; nothing is uploaded or shared.
