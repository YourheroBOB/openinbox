# 這個人是誰 / Brief this person

用本機信件快取介紹一位寄件人：怎麼認識、往來多久、最近在談什麼、對方在等你什麼。
只寫信裡看得到的事實，不要推測身分或背景。

Brief the user on one sender from the local mail cache: how long you have been
in touch, what you discuss lately, what they are waiting on.
Only state what the mail shows; do not guess identity or background.

唯讀。不要寄信。
Read-only. Do not send.
